The lab.

BOOTSTATE is an independent technology lab. We conduct research and build systems across artificial intelligence, decentralized systems and software, beginning from first principles.

Method

How our work progresses.

Every project is tracked through three defined states, each with clear criteria for advancing to the next.

Start

A defined problem, a working hypothesis and an accountable owner.

Iterate

A functioning prototype, refined through testing with external users.

Ship

A released, documented and maintained system that others can rely on.
